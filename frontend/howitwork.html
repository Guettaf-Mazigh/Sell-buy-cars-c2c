how it works

At SemsaR, we make it easy for individuals to buy and sell cars directly without middlemen.
Our platform is simple, secure, and designed for a smooth experience.
## **For Sellers** ###
**1. List Your Car**
- Create an account and log in.
- Click on **"addCar"** and fill in details like car make, model, year, price, and condition.
- Upload high-quality images to attract buyers.
- Submit your listing
- it will go live once approved.
### **2. Receive Offers**
- Interested buyers will contact you via chat, phone, or email.
- Negotiate the price directly with buyers.

## **For Buyers** ###
**1. Browse Listings**
- Use filters (make, model, year, price, ) to find the perfect car.
- Check detailed descriptions.
**2. Contact the Seller**
- call the seller to ask questions.
- Arrange a meeting to inspect the car and take a test drive. ###
**3. Buy with Confidence**
- Negotiate the final price with the seller.
- Ensure proper documentation (title, registration, bill of sale).
- Complete the payment and drive your new car home!

✅ **No Middlemen**

✅ **Secure Transactions**
